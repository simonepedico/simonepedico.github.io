    04 / RESEARCH LAB

    3DiG
    Research Lab.

    I am part of 3DiG, the 3D Interpretation and Digital Geometry research laboratory at the University of Milano-Bicocca’s Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication (DISCo).

    The lab works mainly in computer graphics, developing methods in digital geometry and advanced machine learning for geometric data. Its research spans geometry processing, shape analysis, geometric deep learning, medical applications, and geometric priors for AI.
